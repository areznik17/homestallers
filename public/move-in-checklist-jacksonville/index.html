New home move-in checklist for Jacksonville

What to set up in your first week in a new Jacksonville home, from TVs and furniture to Wi-Fi, locks and hurricane prep.

The first week in a new home goes faster with a plan. Here's what most Jacksonville families set up first, in the order that makes the most sense.

Day one

  • Change or rekey the exterior locks, or add a smart lock
  • Test smoke and CO alarms and note their age
  • Find the main water shutoff and the electrical panel
  • Get Wi-Fi running so everything else can connect

First week

  • Build beds first, then dressers and desks
  • Mount TVs before furniture gets pushed against the walls
  • Hang blinds or blackout shades in bedrooms
  • Set up a video doorbell and cameras
  • Anchor tall furniture if you have kids

First month

  • Hang mirrors, art and shelves once you know where furniture lives
  • Pressure wash the driveway and walkways
  • Put together a hurricane kit before June
