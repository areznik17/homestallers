What counts as one item

A dresser, a bed frame, a desk, a bookcase, a TV stand or a patio set each count as one. Very large pieces like PAX wardrobes, bunk beds and sectionals with lots of modules may count as two.

Anchored for safety

Tipping furniture is a real danger for small kids. Dressers and bookcases over about 30 inches get anchored to the wall with the kit they came with, or one your pro brings.
