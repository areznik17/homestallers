Furniture assembly you can count on

Tall pieces get anchored to the wall, which matters a lot in homes with kids.

Homestallers is led by local, experienced professional Alex Montalbano, with trusted local pros who show up on time, treat your home with care, explain what they did and clean up before they leave.

What counts as one item

A dresser, a bed frame, a desk, a bookcase, a TV stand or a patio set each count as one. Very large pieces like PAX wardrobes, bunk beds and sectionals with lots of modules may count as two.

Anchored for safety

Tipping furniture is a real danger for small kids. Dressers and bookcases over about 30 inches get anchored to the wall with the kit they came with, or one your pro brings.

Office and outdoor too

Standing desks, office chairs, filing cabinets, grills, patio furniture, play sets and storage sheds under 8x8.

Built for Jacksonville homes

Garages in Jacksonville can top 100 degrees in summer, so we build furniture inside where it'll live. Tall pieces get anchored, which also helps keep them steady during storm season.

  • Break down boxes and take them to your bins

Not included

How pricing works

Furniture assembly is a flat $80 per item. It includes up to 1 hour of work for each one, which covers most jobs. If yours needs more time, it's $80 an hour after that, billed in 30 minute steps, and we ask before going over. Parts and materials are extra, at cost.

The price is the same anywhere in our area. There's no travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville, and a flat $35 travel fee from 11 to 30 miles out, like Jacksonville Beach, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Fleming Island and Orange Park.

Frequently asked questions

How much is furniture assembly in Jacksonville?

Do you anchor dressers?

Is there a travel fee?

No travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville. From 11 to 30 miles out, there's a flat $35 travel fee. You'll see it at checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay online when you book, with Apple Pay, PayPal, Google Pay or card. A 3% service fee is added at checkout. If the job needs extra time, you approve and pay for it in the app.

Each price includes the time most jobs take. If yours needs more, it's $80 an hour after, and we ask before going over. We can also come back another day.
