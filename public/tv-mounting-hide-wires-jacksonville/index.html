TV mounting with hidden wires you can count on

HDMI and low-voltage cables run inside the wall. Power uses a UL-listed plug-in cord kit that plugs into your existing outlet, so there are no changes to your home's wiring.

Homestallers is led by local, experienced professional Alex Montalbano, with trusted local pros who show up on time, treat your home with care, explain what they did and clean up before they leave.

How the wires get hidden

Your pro cuts two small openings, one behind the TV and one near the floor where your devices sit. The cables run through the wall between them, and both openings get clean plates. With the plug-in power kit, the TV's power runs through the same path and plugs into the outlet you already have.

Paintable cord covers

On block walls, or when you'd rather not cut the wall, a slim paintable cord cover runs neatly from the TV to the floor. Once it's painted to match, it almost disappears.

Soundbars and devices

Tell us what you have: soundbar, cable box, Apple TV, game console. Your pro plans the cable runs so everything reaches and nothing looks crowded.

Built for Jacksonville homes

Concrete block is common on exterior walls across Jacksonville, which is why we often use a slim paintable cord cover there and in-wall kits on interior drywall. Either way, nothing hangs down the wall.

Yes, when it's done with a UL-listed in-wall cord kit. Regular extension cords should never go inside a wall.
