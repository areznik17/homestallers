What to expect when you book Homestallers

How booking, arrival, extra time, payment and cleanup work when you book a job with Homestallers in Jacksonville.

Here's exactly how a Homestallers visit works, from booking to cleanup.

Booking

Pick your job, choose a time and check out online. You'll get a confirmation email right away, and a text the day before.

Arrival

Your pro arrives in the window you picked, walks through the job with you and confirms the plan before starting.

The work

Every package includes a set amount of time. If the job needs more, we tell you first. Extra time is $80 an hour, and you decide whether we keep going or come back another day.

Cleanup and payment

We clean up, haul boxes to your bins and walk you through what we did. Any extra time or parts are paid the same day, in cash or online.

After the job

If anything we installed doesn't hold or work right within 30 days because of our work, we come back and fix it at no labor charge.
