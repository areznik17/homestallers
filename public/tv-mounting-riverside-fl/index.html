Picking the right height

The center of the screen should sit about at eye level when you're seated, usually 42 to 48 inches off the floor for a living room. Over a fireplace it ends up higher, so a tilting or pull-down mount helps. Your pro will hold the TV up so you can see it before anything gets drilled.

Which mount to buy

Fixed mounts sit flattest against the wall. Tilting mounts help when the TV is up high. Full-motion arms are best for corners or rooms where you watch from different spots. Check that the mount's VESA pattern and weight rating match your TV. Not sure? Your pro can bring one at cost.

Drywall, block and brick

Most Jacksonville homes have drywall over wood studs, but many older homes and lots of exterior walls are concrete block. Your pro brings the right anchors for both, so you don't have to guess.
