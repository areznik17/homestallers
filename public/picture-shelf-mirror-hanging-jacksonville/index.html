Gallery walls

Lay your frames out on the floor or tape paper templates on the wall first. Your pro can help you settle the spacing, usually 2 to 3 inches between frames, then hangs everything level.

Heavy mirrors and art

Heavy pieces go into studs or onto rated anchors and French cleats. Block walls get masonry anchors.

Built for Jacksonville homes

Older homes have plaster and newer ones have drywall, while exterior walls are often block. Humidity can also loosen cheap adhesive hooks, so we hang with real hardware.

Frequently asked questions

How much to hang a mirror?

$90 per item. Gallery walls are usually best booked as a handyman hour.

Can you hang on block walls?

Yes, with masonry anchors.

Is there a travel fee?

No travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville. From 11 to 30 miles out, there's a flat $35 travel fee. You'll see it at checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay online when you book, with Apple Pay, PayPal, Google Pay or card. A 3% service fee is added at checkout. If the job needs extra time, you approve and pay for it in the app.

What if the job takes longer?

Each price includes the time most jobs take. If yours needs more, it's $80 an hour after, and we ask before going over. We can also come back another day.

Can I tip?

Yes. You can leave a tip in the app after the job. It's always optional.

What if I need to cancel?
