Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

What we collect

When you book or contact us, we collect your name, phone, email, service address and the details you share about the job. We don't collect more than we need.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

We use your information only to schedule and do your job, send confirmations and receipts, and follow up about your service. We don't sell or rent your information.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business: Stripe for payments, our email provider for confirmations, and our website host. Each only gets what it needs.

Your location

If you tap "Use my location," your browser shares your approximate location once to check whether you're in our area. We don't store it.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by emailing homestallers@gmail.com. Reply STOP to any text to stop messages.
