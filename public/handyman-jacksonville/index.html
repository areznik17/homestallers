How pricing works

Handyman hour is a flat $80 per item. It includes up to 1 hour of work for each one, which covers most jobs. If yours needs more time, it's $80 an hour after that, billed in 30 minute steps, and we ask before going over. Parts and materials are extra, at cost.

The price is the same anywhere in our area. There's no travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville, and a flat $35 travel fee from 11 to 30 miles out, like Jacksonville Beach, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Fleming Island and Orange Park.

Frequently asked questions

What does a handyman cost in Jacksonville?

$80 an hour, 1 hour minimum, plus parts at cost.

Can you do several small jobs in one visit?

Yes. That's the best way to use the time.

What can't you do?

Electrical and plumbing work. Those services are coming soon, done by a licensed contractor.

Is there a travel fee?

No travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville. From 11 to 30 miles out, there's a flat $35 travel fee. You'll see it at checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay online when you book, with Apple Pay, PayPal, Google Pay or card. A 3% service fee is added at checkout. If the job needs extra time, you approve and pay for it in the app.

What if the job takes longer?

Each price includes the time most jobs take. If yours needs more, it's $80 an hour after, and we ask before going over. We can also come back another day.

Can I tip?

Yes. You can leave a tip in the app after the job. It's always optional.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund.
