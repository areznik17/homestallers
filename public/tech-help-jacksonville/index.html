Wi-Fi that reaches everywhere

Jacksonville homes are often long and spread out, and block walls can stop a signal cold. A mesh Wi-Fi system with two or three units usually fixes dead spots for good. Your pro sets it up, moves your devices over and tests every room.

Help for parents and grandparents

Setting up a new phone or tablet, getting video calls working, cleaning up email, making the TV simple to use. Your pro is patient and explains things in plain English, and can leave a simple written cheat sheet.

New devices, set up right

Printers, streaming boxes, smart speakers, laptops and tablets. Your pro connects them, updates them and makes sure they work together.

Built for Jacksonville homes

Long ranch homes and concrete block walls are common across Jacksonville, and both block Wi-Fi. A mesh system usually fixes dead spots, including out to the lanai and pool.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help my parents with their tech?

Yes. That's one of the most common bookings. You can book for them and add their address.

Do I need to buy equipment first?

Not always. Your pro can tell you what to get, or pick it up at cost.

Can you fix slow Wi-Fi?

Usually. Often it's router placement or a mesh system.

Is there a travel fee?

No travel fee within 10 miles of central Jacksonville. From 11 to 30 miles out, there's a flat $35 travel fee. You'll see it at checkout.

When do I pay?

You pay online when you book, with Apple Pay, PayPal, Google Pay or card. A 3% service fee is added at checkout. If the job needs extra time, you approve and pay for it in the app.

What if the job takes longer?

Each price includes the time most jobs take. If yours needs more, it's $80 an hour after, and we ask before going over. We can also come back another day.

Can I tip?

Yes. You can leave a tip in the app after the job. It's always optional.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel up to 24 hours before for a full refund.
