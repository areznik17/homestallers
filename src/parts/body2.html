Built for Jacksonville homes.

Northeast Florida homes have their own quirks. We plan for them on every job.

Block, plaster and drywall

Concrete block exterior walls, plaster in historic Riverside and San Marco, drywall in new builds. We bring the right anchors for each.

Heat, humidity and salt air

From the Beaches to the river, Florida weather is hard on hardware, caulk and outdoor surfaces. We use materials that hold up.

Storm season ready

June through November, we help with battery-backed cameras, anchored furniture and getting the outside clean and clear.

About us

Homestallers is a Jacksonville home help company led by local, experienced professional Alex Montalbano. We started it because people kept asking for someone they could trust to show up on time and do the job right.

Every job has a flat price you see before you book. We show up when we say we will, treat your home with care and clean up before we leave. If a job needs more time, we ask first.

We mount TVs and hide the wires, set up smart home gear and Wi-Fi, build furniture, hang shelves and blinds, help with moves, pressure wash and knock out the small fixes on your list. We serve Jacksonville and the communities within 30 miles, from the Beaches to Orange Park and from Yulee to St. Johns.
